# FRENCH MERINGUE OPEN 法式蛋白霜

## French Meringue 法式蛋白霜

| Ingrédients | 食材 |
|-------------|------|
| 250 g de egg whites | 250 克蛋清 |
| 250 g de granulated sugar | 250 克细砂糖 |
| 250 g de powdered sugar | 250 克糖粉 |
| 1 g de cream of tartar | 1 克塔塔粉或少量柠檬汁 |

Whip egg whites with acid to soft peaks, add granulated sugar gradually, then fold in powdered sugar.
蛋清与酸打到软峰，分次加入细砂糖打发，最后折入糖粉。适合烘干成脆底、装饰或轻盈结构。
